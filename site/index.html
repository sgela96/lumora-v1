How it works

Stop asking users for screenshots.

Which browser are you on? What did you click? Can you send a screenshot? Most bug reports start with a round of questions. Lumora is a widget you add to your app, and with it the answers arrive with the report.

01Your user

Open the widget

The Lumora widget is a small glowing nebula in the corner of your app. It pulses when the page throws an error, so people notice it at the right moment.

  • Reports from users who would never open a ticket
  • Errors noticed as they happen
  • Your colors, English or Spanish

02Your user

Show the problem

They take a screenshot and mark it up, or record their screen and talk through what happened. Anything private can be blacked out first.

  • Highlight, draw and hide data
  • Screen recording with voice, up to 10 minutes
  • No browser permissions for screenshots

03Lumora

Lumora writes it up

While they record, Lumora notes each click and page change and turns them into numbered steps. Console errors, failed requests and browser details are attached automatically.

  • Steps with timestamps
  • Errors shown on the step where they happened
  • Typed values are never recorded
04Your team

Get a ticket you can work on

The ticket is created in Jira, GitHub, Zendesk or wherever your team works, using your template, with the screenshot or video attached.

  • Your template, formatted for each tool
  • Status changes come back to Lumora
  • Repeat reports grouped together
